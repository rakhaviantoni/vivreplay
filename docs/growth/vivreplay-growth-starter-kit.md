# VivrePlay growth starter kit

Working version: 13 October 2026

This is the first working content kit for VivrePlay. It uses product details visible in the project: a One Piece Card Game card catalog, EN and JP printings, deck building, a signed-in Vault for collections, community Market listings, and recorded tournament meta. Confirm availability in production before publishing any feature-specific post. Do not publish changing catalog counts, seller counts, prices, or performance claims without checking live data.

## Positioning

**For One Piece Card Game players and collectors, VivrePlay is a place to look up the exact card, build a deck, keep a collection, and find community listings.**

Use this short version in profiles and introductions:

> Your One Piece Card Game library: cards, decks, collection, and community listings.

The clearest entry point is card discovery. Lead with finding the right card or printing. Show deck building, Vault, and Market as useful next steps, not as a list of unrelated features.

## Who we are speaking to

- **Deck builders:** need to check card text and printings, compare options, and turn ideas into a deck list.
- **Collectors:** need to identify EN and JP versions and keep track of cards, including graded slabs where supported.
- **Players following the competitive scene:** want to explore recorded tournament matches and meta lists.
- **Buyers and sellers:** want listings tied to the specific card printing, with condition and seller details visible.

## Voice

Write like a knowledgeable player who helps someone get to the right card faster. Be specific, curious, and relaxed. Use card codes, set names, or a real deck question when they add value. Invite replies that players can answer from experience.

Avoid claims that VivrePlay is official, has the biggest or most complete database, guarantees the best price, or has a particular number of users or listings. VivrePlay is an independent community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bandai. Point players to official Bandai sources for current rules, errata, and tournament rulings.

## Ready-to-make posts

Use screen recordings or current product screenshots. Keep card art and card text legible. Only use artwork that VivrePlay has permission to publish on social channels; otherwise record the interface with card art obscured or use approved assets.

### Post 1: Find the exact printing

**Format:** 15-second screen recording, card search to printing details.

**On-screen text:** Same card name. Different printing. Check the version first.

**Caption, English:** Looking for a specific EN or JP printing? Search by card and check the printing details before you add it to a list or collection. Try the card catalog at vivreplay.com/cards.

**Caption, Bahasa Indonesia:** Sedang mencari versi cetak EN atau JP tertentu? Cari kartunya, lalu cek detail cetakan sebelum menambahkannya ke deck atau koleksi. Coba katalog kartu di vivreplay.com/cards.

**Prompt:** Which printing are you hunting for?

### Post 2: From card to deck

**Format:** Carousel: card search, card detail, deck builder, finished list. Use a real current flow.

**Slide 1:** Found a card. What goes around it?

**Slide 2:** Check the card details.

**Slide 3:** Add your choices in the deck builder.

**Slide 4:** Review the list and keep tuning.

**Caption, English:** A deck idea often starts with one card. Look it up, test the surrounding choices, then shape the list in the VivrePlay deck builder. What card is at the center of your current build?

**Caption, Bahasa Indonesia:** Ide deck sering dimulai dari satu kartu. Cari kartunya, cek pilihan pendukung, lalu susun daftar di deck builder VivrePlay. Kartu apa yang jadi pusat deck Anda sekarang?

### Post 3: Make your collection easier to check

**Format:** 20-second Vault walkthrough. Show only fields currently available.

**On-screen text:** Your binder, easier to search.

**Caption, English:** Keep the cards you own in your VivrePlay Vault, then use the list when you are planning a deck or checking what you still need. Sign in to save your collection.

**Caption, Bahasa Indonesia:** Catat kartu yang Anda miliki di Vault VivrePlay. Gunakan daftarnya saat menyusun deck atau mengecek kartu yang masih dicari. Masuk untuk menyimpan koleksi.

**Prompt:** Do you track your collection in an app, a spreadsheet, or a binder?

### Post 4: Follow a recorded match

**Format:** Carousel or short clip using one verified match page from `/meta`.

**On-screen text:** What did the finalists bring?

**Caption, English:** Pick a recorded match and follow the deck lists and game details. Start with the match page, then tell us which card choice caught your eye. Match records can be incomplete, so check the event and list details on the page.

**Caption, Bahasa Indonesia:** Pilih pertandingan yang tercatat, lalu lihat deck list dan detail gamenya. Mulai dari halaman pertandingan, lalu ceritakan pilihan kartu yang menarik perhatian Anda. Data pertandingan bisa belum lengkap, jadi cek detail event dan deck list di halaman tersebut.

### Post 5: Browse a listing by printing

**Format:** Screen recording from Market search to one listing. Hide personal data and confirm the listing is active.

**On-screen text:** Check the printing. Read the condition. Then decide.

**Caption, English:** Shopping for a particular version? Browse Market listings by printing, then read the condition, language, seller, and price on the listing before you decide. Availability and prices can change.

**Caption, Bahasa Indonesia:** Sedang mencari versi tertentu? Jelajahi listing Market berdasarkan cetakan, lalu periksa kondisi, bahasa, penjual, dan harga sebelum memutuskan. Ketersediaan dan harga bisa berubah.

### Post 6: Community question

**Format:** Text-led story or simple post using a neutral background and no unlicensed card art.

**Prompt, English:** What makes you keep a card: the art, the deck it fits, or the memory attached to it?

**Prompt, Bahasa Indonesia:** Apa yang membuat Anda menyimpan sebuah kartu: ilustrasinya, deck yang cocok dengannya, atau kenangan di balik kartu itu?

**Follow-up:** Share a few answers with permission and ask each player to name the card. Link the card catalog when the card is available there.

## Search-friendly article backlog

Write these only when the pages and examples can support the article. Use original explanations, and link to official Bandai material for current rules or errata.

1. **How to check One Piece Card Game card printings in EN and JP**: use catalog search and explain which printing details to compare.
2. **How to start a One Piece Card Game deck list**: show the VivrePlay builder with a real sample list and point to official rules for construction rules.
3. **A simple way to track your One Piece Card Game collection**: demonstrate the Vault and state which details it currently stores.
4. **How to read a One Piece Card Game Market listing**: explain the visible printing, condition, language, seller, and price details. Do not promise buyer protection beyond published policy.
5. **What a recorded tournament deck list can and cannot tell you**: show a real VivrePlay match record, note gaps, and direct readers to official sources for current rulings.

## Simple measurement loop

Record the post date, language, format, topic, reach, saves, shares, comments, profile visits, link clicks, and visits to the destination page. Compare posts within the same format where possible. Use link tags for campaign links if analytics supports them. Treat comments and saves as signals of usefulness, while link clicks and the next product action show whether the post brought people into VivrePlay.

At the end of two weeks, keep the two formats with the strongest combination of saves, shares, and qualified visits. Make another version of those. Retire formats that get attention without product visits unless they serve a clear community goal.

## Publishing checks

- Confirm the destination page and feature work in production on mobile.
- Verify any match, deck list, Market listing, price, or card data shown in the creative.
- Use only artwork and screenshots approved for the channel and purpose.
- Check the current VivrePlay terms and independent-project disclosure.
- Remove personal information from account, order, and seller screenshots.
- Use real performance data. Do not invent counts, outcomes, player quotes, or endorsements.
